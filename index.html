01 · Problem → Solution

New employees get lost in business software

❌ Problem

  • CRM and ERP systems have hundreds of menus and forms. A new employee opens the program and doesn't know where anything is.
  • It takes about two weeks before a new CRM employee can work alone (measured at our pilot client).
  • Senior staff are constantly interrupted with the same "where do I click?" questions.
  • Manuals and training videos go out of date and don't match the real screen.

✅ Solution: SCOPOS

  • The employee asks in their own words: "How do I create an invoice?"
  • SCOPOS reads the live screen and points the cursor at the next click, step by step.
  • It works on top of the company's existing system. Nothing needs to be re-implemented.
  • It's a coach, not a bot: the person clicks and learns. Russian, Uzbek and English.
