Pitch Day 3.0 · Windows application for business teams

01 · Problem → Solution

New employees get lost in business software

❌ Problem

  • CRM and ERP systems have hundreds of menus and forms. A new employee opens the program and doesn't know where anything is.
  • At our pilot company, a new CRM employee needs about two weeks before working without help.
  • Senior staff are interrupted again and again with the same "where do I click?" questions.
  • Manuals and training videos go out of date and don't match the real screen.

✅ Solution: SCOPOS

  • The employee asks in their own words: "How do I create an invoice?"
  • SCOPOS reads the live screen and points the cursor at the next click, step by step.
  • It sits on top of the company's existing system. Nothing has to be re-implemented.
  • A coach, not a bot: the person clicks and learns. Russian, Uzbek and English.

05 · Implementation plan

How we build it

StageWhatTechnologies, tools & AI
1. See ✓Read the screen and the structure of the open windowScreen capture + Windows UI Automation (names and positions of buttons)
2. Understand ✓Turn the question into a route of stepsClaude vision model gets the screenshot and the list of on-screen controls together and returns a structured step plan
3. Point ✓Show each step with a cursor on the exact buttonTransparent overlay; each step is matched to a real control by name; re-plans when the screen changes
4. Remember ✓Learn a route once, replay it instantlySaved routes + company knowledge base; repeated questions need no AI call
5. Pilot → paid · nowProve faster onboarding at U VENDOR, convert to a contractUsage analytics: where employees get stuck
6. Private mode · 2027Run on the client's own server for sensitive dataFine-tuned local vision model (planned)
7. Scale · 2027Sell through CRM/ERP integrators, then KazakhstanReady-made guide packs per system
