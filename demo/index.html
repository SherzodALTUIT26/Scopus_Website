6.2 · What the video shows

A new employee asks SCOPOS instead of a senior colleague

Length: 1:46

  1. 0:00 The problem. Day one at a new job: a CRM full of menus. Every question interrupts a senior, and manuals never match the screen.
  2. 0:26 Just ask. The employee opens SCOPOS and types: "How do I create an invoice for a client?"
  3. 0:32 Step by step. SCOPOS reads the screen and the blue cursor points at each button in turn: Sales → Invoices → New invoice → choose client, add items, save. The employee clicks, SCOPOS follows the screen.
  4. 1:10 Result. The invoice is done in 40 seconds without asking anyone. Routes SCOPOS has learned replay in 0.4 s.
  5. 1:26 How it works. Reads the interface → understands the question → points to the exact button. A coach, not a bot: the person always clicks.

The CRM in the video is a neutral mock-up. SCOPOS works on top of any Windows or web business software.
