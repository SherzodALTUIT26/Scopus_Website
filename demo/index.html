6.2 · What the video shows

A new employee asks SCOPOS instead of a senior colleague

Length: 1:46

  1. 0:00 The problem. Day one at a new job: a CRM full of menus. Every question interrupts a senior, and manuals never match the screen.
  2. 0:26 Just ask. The employee opens SCOPOS and types: "How do I create an invoice for a client?"
  3. 0:32 Step by step. SCOPOS reads the screen and the blue cursor points at each button in turn: Sales → Invoices → New invoice → choose client, add items, save. The employee clicks, SCOPOS follows the screen.
  4. 1:10 Result. The invoice is done in 40 seconds without asking anyone. Routes SCOPOS has already learned replay instantly.
  5. 1:26 How it works. Reads the interface → understands the question → points to the exact button. A coach, not a bot: the person always clicks.

The CRM in the video is a neutral mock-up, so the demo is not tied to one vendor. The prototype is being tested on our pilot company's real CRM.

Optional · AI assistant examples

Example questions and answers

"How do I create an invoice for a client?"

Steps with the cursor: Sales → Invoices → + New invoice → choose the client, add items → Save.

"Where do I find a client's order history?"

Steps with the cursor: Clients → search the client by name → open the card → Orders tab.

"How do I change a deal's status?"

Steps with the cursor: Deals → open the deal → Status dropdown → choose the new status → Save.

"What does the Warehouse section do?"

Text answer: it shows stock levels and incoming and outgoing goods; SCOPOS then offers to point to it on the screen.
