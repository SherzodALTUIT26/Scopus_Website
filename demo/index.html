6.2 · What the video shows

A new employee opens the CRM for the first time

  1. A new worker opens the company's CRM and doesn't understand where anything is — dozens of menus, tabs and buttons.
  2. Instead of asking a colleague, they open SCOPOS (hotkey) and type a question in plain language.
  3. SCOPOS captures the screen, reads the window's interface structure and builds a step-by-step route.
  4. A blue cursor flies to the exact button for step 1, with a short instruction card next to it.
  5. The worker clicks; SCOPOS sees the screen change and moves the cursor to the next step — until the task is done.

SCOPOS never clicks for the user — it teaches. After a few questions the employee knows the program on their own, and repeated routes are replayed instantly from memory without an AI call.
