SCOPOS

▶ Watch the demo Product website GitHub
01 · Problem → Solution

New employees get lost in corporate software

❌ Problem

  • CRM and ERP systems (1C, Bitrix24, amoCRM) have hundreds of menus and forms. A new worker opens the program and does not know where anything is.
  • Onboarding takes weeks; senior staff are constantly interrupted with the same "where do I click?" questions.
  • Written instructions and videos go out of date and don't match what the person actually sees on the screen.
  • Mistakes in documents and orders cost the company money.

✅ Solution — SCOPOS

  • The worker types a question: "How do I create a sales invoice?"
  • SCOPOS reads the screen (screenshot + Windows UI Automation tree) and builds a step-by-step route.
  • A blue cursor flies to the exact button for each step; the next step appears when the screen changes.
  • It is a coach, not a bot: the person clicks themselves and learns the program. Works in Russian, Uzbek and English, across multiple monitors.
